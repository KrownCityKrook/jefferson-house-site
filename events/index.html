The Space

One courtyard, and everything around it.

Jefferson House is an open-air venue at 83rd Avenue and Jefferson Street in Downtown Peoria's Arts District. The courtyard is the room — a covered black-steel canopy over the whole thing, with heaters, fans and misters so it works in February and in July. The kitchens, shops, bar and stage all open onto it.

You can take the courtyard on its own, or take the whole property. We're a new venue, so we have real flexibility on how a night gets shaped — and we'd rather build the layout around your event than hand you a package.

What People Book It For

Day events and night events, both.

The courtyard reads differently depending on when you use it. Mornings and afternoons it's bright and open and good for markets, showers and family things. After dark the canopy lighting and the stage take over. We're glad to do either, and plenty of events use both halves of the day.

Event Questions

The things people ask first.

How many people fit?

The courtyard seats 300+ and holds more standing. The Jefferson Street patio works for smaller groups and can be held privately on its own. We'll size it with you once we know the shape of the event.

Can we get married here?

Yes. The courtyard works for a ceremony and a reception in the same place, and the stage handles the band or the DJ. We're a new venue rather than a wedding factory, so expect a conversation rather than a tier sheet.

Do we have to use your kitchens?

No, but three of them are right here and run by the people who own them, so it's usually the easier and better answer. Tell us what you're imagining and we'll talk it through.

Is it indoors or outdoors?

Open-air, under a covered black-steel canopy, with heaters, fans and misters. It's built for Arizona weather in both directions — you're outside, but you're not exposed.

Can we book a daytime event?

Absolutely, and we'd like more of them. Mornings and afternoons in the courtyard are bright and open, which suits markets, showers, brunches and family gatherings.

What about parking?

Public surface lots and on-street parking surround the venue throughout Downtown Peoria. Guests park once and walk — no venue lot to manage, no valet required.

How far out are you booking?

We open in November 2026 and are taking inquiries for late 2026 and 2027. Booking early means more input on how your event gets set up.
