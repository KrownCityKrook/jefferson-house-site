Where We Are

83rd Avenue and Jefferson Street.

8310 W. Jefferson Street, Peoria, AZ 85345. Northwest corner of 83rd Avenue and Jefferson, in the heart of Downtown Peoria's Arts District — just off Grand Avenue and a few minutes from the Loop 101.

If you grew up calling this part of town Old Town, it's the same place. The City calls it Downtown Peoria now, and the historic core here was platted back in 1886.

Parking

It's free, and it's right there.

Public surface lots and on-street parking surround the venue throughout the downtown district. There's no venue lot to fight over and no valet — park once and walk, which is rather the point of a downtown.

If the block right out front is full on a Saturday night, the lots a street over almost never are.

What It's Like

Outside, but not exposed.

The whole courtyard sits under a covered black-steel canopy with heaters, fans and misters, so it works in February and it works in July. You're outdoors the entire time — there's no indoor dining room to retreat into, and that's deliberate.

No reservation is required and there's no cover to get in. Come for one thing and stay for three, or grab a coffee and leave. Both are fine.

If the courtyard is livelier than you want, the patio along Jefferson Street sits a little apart from it, with trees and bistro lights over the tables.

Bringing kids? The courtyard is all ages and the 16-foot splash pad was built for them. Some evening events are 21+, and we'll say so clearly when they are.

Accessibility

Wide paths, and two steps you should know about.

The tenant level and the courtyard floor are not quite on the same plane. There are two small steps down into the courtyard — a step, not a staircase, but if you use a wheelchair or you're pushing a stroller, that's the thing worth knowing before you drive out.

Once you're in the courtyard it's wide and even, with room between the kitchens, shops, bar and stage for a chair or a stroller to move comfortably on a busy night.

We're confirming the accessible route into the courtyard with the architect and we'll publish it here once it's settled, rather than describe it now and get it wrong. If you need a definite answer before then, email hello@jeffersonhouseaz.com and we'll find out for you instead of guessing.

The Rest of the Block

We're one stop, not the whole trip.

Downtown Peoria has been rebuilding for a while and we're a recent arrival to it. A few neighbors worth your time while you're here:

  • Peoria Center for the Performing Arts 10580 N 83rd Drive — the City's performing arts center, home to Theater Works. A show there and dinner here is an easy night.
  • Caldwell County BBQ Washington Street between 83rd Avenue and 83rd Drive — opened April 2026, and approved by City Council the same season we were.
  • Driftwood Coffee Co. 8295 W Jefferson Street — a block away, and run by some of the same people building this place.
  • Fire House Event Center 8307 W Washington Street — a long-running event space around the corner.
