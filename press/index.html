The Site

Why this corner.

The historic core of Downtown Peoria was platted in 1886, and the railway that made it — the Santa Fe, Prescott & Phoenix — came through in 1895. The shipping containers on the property are a nod to that. The black-steel canopy over the courtyard is a nod to the ironworks that stood here before.

One note on naming: the City of Peoria calls this district Downtown Peoria, and so do we. You will also see it called Old Town — same place, older habit.
