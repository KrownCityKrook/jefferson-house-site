A House for the West Side

Downtown's been here since 1886. It was due a porch.

Jefferson House is a partnership between the people behind The Churchill in downtown Phoenix and Peoria's own Banquet hospitality group — built hand-in-hand with the City of Peoria as part of its investment in the historic Downtown core.

The bones tell the neighborhood's story: shipping containers nod to the 1895 railway that put Peoria on the map, and the black-steel canopy tips its hat to the ironworks that stood here before us. Under it all — three chef-run kitchens, six retail and eatery spots, one good bar, a live stage, a splash pad for the kids, and seats for three hundred of our closest friends. Heaters, fans and misters keep the courtyard good all year. This is desert living, after all.

Good Questions

When does Jefferson House open?

We're under construction now and targeting Fall 2026. Follow @jeffersonhouseaz for the latest.

Is it family-friendly?

Very. The courtyard is open to all ages and the 16-foot splash pad was built for the kids. Some evening events may be 21+.

Good spot for a date?

Built for it. Something cold from the bar, something sweet from the shops, live music on the stage — and no reservation required. Park once, stay a while.

Can I book an event before you open?

Yes — we're taking private event and live music inquiries for late 2026 and 2027 now. Tell us about your event.

How do I lease a space?

Three turnkey kitchen suites (640 SF) and six retail & eatery spots (160–440 SF) are leasing. Apply here or book an intro meeting with the team.

Are you hiring?

We will be staffing up ahead of opening — bar, floor, security and management. See positions and apply.
